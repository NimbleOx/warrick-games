    How to play

    • Your line never stops. Steer with ←→ or AD, or hold the left or right half of the screen.
    • Hit a wall or any trail, including your own, and you're out.
    • Every so often your trail leaves a gap. Anyone can slip through it.
    • Each time someone crashes, everyone still alive gets a point.
