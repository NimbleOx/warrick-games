    How to play

    • ↑↓ drive, ←→ turn, Space fire. On a phone: the stick and the Fire button.
    • Tap a turn key to nudge your aim; hold it to spin.
    • You have 5 bullets. Each bounces off walls for 6 seconds, then you get it back.
    • One hit kills — and that includes your own bullets coming back.
    • Last tank standing scores a point. A new maze every round.
